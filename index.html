Privacy Policy for GST Billing and Invoicing

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GST Billing and Invoicing handles information when you use the application. The app helps businesses prepare invoices, calculate totals and applicable taxes, configure printing, and share billing documents.

1. Information You Enter

Depending on the features you use, you may enter information such as:

  • Business details, including shop name, address, phone number, email address and GSTIN.
  • Customer details that you choose to enter.
  • Product descriptions, quantities, prices, tax amounts and invoice totals.
  • UPI details or QR-code information used on invoices.
  • Printer preferences and configuration details.

This information may include personal or business information about you, your customers or other people. Please enter only information that you are authorized to use.

2. How Information Is Used

Information you enter is used to provide the app's billing features, prepare invoices, calculate totals and taxes, configure printing, generate billing documents and support other features you choose to use.

3. Data Storage

Business and billing information that you save in GST Billing and Invoicing is stored locally on your device. The app does not currently use a separate online database to store this information.

Local information may remain on your device so you can use it again. The exact information retained depends on the features you use and what you save.

If you export or share an invoice, a copy may be created or transferred outside the app. Copies saved or shared separately may not be deleted when information is removed from the app.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

The app does not currently send your business and billing records to a separate online database as part of its normal data storage.

If you choose to share an invoice or PDF using another application, the information in that document will be shared with the recipient or service you select. That service will handle the information according to its own terms and privacy policy.

The app does not require you to create an account or log in.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription or other digital product through Google Play, Google Play processes the transaction under its own terms and privacy practices. The app may receive purchase status and related information needed to verify purchases and provide access to paid features.

Payment card or bank details used for a Google Play purchase are handled by Google Play and its payment providers, rather than being stored by the app as part of its local billing records.

6. Analytics and Advertising

The developer does not currently use analytics or crash-reporting services as part of the app's intended functionality. This statement should be reviewed if third-party libraries or services are added or changed in a future version.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information saved locally may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android device behavior and any copies you have exported or shared.

Uninstalling the app will not necessarily delete PDF invoices or other copies that you saved elsewhere or sent to other people. You may need to delete those copies separately.

8. Data Security

Keeping information on your device means that its security also depends on your device's security settings and who can access your device. Use a device lock and avoid sharing invoices containing sensitive information with unintended recipients.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

GST Billing and Invoicing is a business utility and is not designed specifically for children. Users should not enter children's personal information unless it is necessary and they are authorized to do so.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this webpage, with an updated revision date.
